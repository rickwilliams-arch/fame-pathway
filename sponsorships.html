SCENE 01 // WHY PARTNER WITH FAME

Partnership Impact

Every sponsorship directly funds camera packages, studio lighting, podcast microphones, editing workstations, and student film festival submissions.

Community Reach & Student Opportunity

Real-World Media Production Powered by Local Sponsors

When your business sponsors the FAME Pathway at Floyd County College & Career Academy, you aren’t just placing a logo—you are hiring a next-generation student creative agency. Our crews produce custom commercials, podcast segments, studio signage, and social media features seen by families, students, and community leaders across Rome and Floyd County.

With over 300,000+ annual views across FAME’s YouTube and Instagram channels—plus live visibility on our studio set and at The FAMECCA Awards—your partnership delivers year-round brand recognition while directly supporting career-ready CTAE education.

Potential Tax-Deductible Contribution

Contributions supporting public Career, Technical & Agricultural Education (CTAE) programs through Floyd County Schools may qualify as tax-deductible charitable or promotional business expenses. Official documentation is provided upon sponsorship confirmation.
