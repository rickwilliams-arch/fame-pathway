SECTION 01 // OVERVIEW & PRODUCTIONS

Course Description

Welcome to the Film, Arts, Media & Entertainment (FAME) Pathway at Floyd County Schools College & Career Academy.

Hands-On Studio & Career Immersion

The FAME Pathway immerses high school students in real-world television broadcasting, narrative filmmaking, podcasting, live multi-camera production, audio engineering, and digital media storytelling. Operating like a working creative agency and broadcast studio, students rotate through authentic crew roles—including Director, Producer, Cinematographer, Audio Engineer, Editor, Talent, and Floor Manager.

Throughout the year, students collaborate across school systems and community partners to plan, shoot, edit, and publish flagship productions seen by thousands of viewers across Floyd County and beyond.

Signature FAME Productions

BROADCAST NEWS

FCS Flash News

Our fast-paced student-produced district news broadcast spotlighting student achievements, campus events, athletics, and community stories across Floyd County Schools.

Anchor Desk • Field Reporting • Live Switching
ENTERTAINMENT SERIES

FAME or FAIL

An energetic, student-created variety and challenge show blending comedy, rapid-fire trivia, creative studio segments, and unscripted entertainment for high engagement.

Multi-Cam Studio • Graphics • High-Energy Editing
AUDIO & VIDEO PODCAST

The FAME Project Podcasts

Long-form interviews and roundtable discussions with community leaders, educators, artists, and students—recorded with broadcast microphones and multi-angle cinema cameras.

Rodecaster Pro II • Interviewing • Sponsor Spots

SECTION 02 // LEARNING FRAMEWORK

Course Objectives

Every project in FAME is anchored by three core learning pillars that develop technical mastery and workplace readiness.

PILLAR GROUP 01

The Foundational Four

Core Technical Craft

  • 01. Pre-Production & Storytelling: Scriptwriting, storyboarding, shot lists, and production planning.
  • 02. Camera & Lighting: Composition, exposure, camera movement, and three-point studio lighting.
  • 03. Audio Acquisition & Design: Mic placement, live mixing, voiceover recording, and soundscapes.
  • 04. Post-Production & Delivery: Non-linear video editing, color correction, motion graphics, and mastering.
PILLAR GROUP 02

The Four Pillars of FAME

Creative Disciplines

  • F // Film: Narrative cinema, short films, documentary storytelling, and visual directing.
  • A // Arts: Graphic design, set aesthetics, photography, and visual branding.
  • M // Media: Broadcast journalism, podcasting, social media strategy, and digital publishing.
  • E // Entertainment: Live event coverage, variety programming, audience engagement, and showrunning.
PILLAR GROUP 03

The Four C’s of FAME

Professional Studio Habits

  • C1. Creativity: Originating bold, authentic concepts and solving visual challenges on set.
  • C2. Collaboration: Working seamlessly across production crews where every role is vital.
  • C3. Communication: Pitching ideas clearly, giving constructive feedback, and executing crew cues.
  • C4. Consistency: Meeting broadcast deadlines and maintaining high quality on every shoot.

CAREER & COLLEGE READINESS OUTCOME

Digital Demo Reel & Portfolio Readiness

By the end of the FAME Pathway, every student graduates with a curated digital portfolio and demo reel showcasing published broadcasts, commercials, podcasts, and short films—ready for college film programs, internships, and entry-level media careers.

SECTION 03 // CURRICULUM & STUDIO GEAR

Course Content Overview

Sequential CTAE pathway courses paired with professional cinema, broadcast, and audio production equipment.

COURSE 01 // INTRODUCTORY YEAR 1

Audio Visual Technology & Film I

Introduces foundational studio safety, camera operation, shot composition, basic lighting, microphone fundamentals, script formatting, and introductory non-linear editing in Adobe Premiere Pro.

COURSE 02 // INTERMEDIATE YEAR 2

Audio Visual Technology & Film II

Expands into multi-camera studio switching, narrative short filmmaking, advanced lighting setups, multi-track audio mixing in Audition, client commercial production, and active crew roles on FAME shows.

COURSE 03 // PATHWAY COMPLETER YEAR 3

Audio Visual Technology & Film III

Focuses on executive producing, directing, advanced post-production, festival-ready cinema projects, End-of-Pathway Assessment (EOPA) preparation, and building a professional demo reel.

COURSE 04 // ADVANCED PRACTICUM ADVANCED

Broadcast Video Production

Senior-level studio practicum leading daily broadcast operations, showrunning FCS Flash News, FAME or FAIL, and The FAME Project Podcasts, and mentoring incoming student crews.

SECTION 04 // ASSESSMENT BREAKDOWN

Grading Policy

Grades reflect both daily studio professionalism and completed media productions.

CATEGORY 01

25%

Participation

Active engagement in studio crew roles, daily readiness, equipment care, and collaborative teamwork.

CATEGORY 02

30%

Project Completion

Executing pre-production plans, filming required coverage, and delivering complete rough and final cuts.

CATEGORY 03

20%

Timeliness of Work

Meeting broadcast air dates, script check-ins, and editing milestones on schedule.

CATEGORY 04

25%

Portfolio & Final Edits

Technical polish, audio/color quality, rubric mastery, and curated digital portfolio updates.

BROADCAST DEADLINES & LATE ASSIGNMENT POLICY

In media and television production, air dates are non-negotiable. Assignments submitted after the published deadline without prior instructor communication or an excused absence will incur a 10% daily point deduction (up to 3 days) and must be coordinated directly with Mr. Williams.

SECTION 05 // STUDIO STANDARDS

Class Policies & Expectations

Our classroom operates as a professional media production facility. Respect for people, time, and equipment is mandatory.

Attendance & Punctuality

Arrive on time and ready for crew call. When absent, check Google Classroom and coordinate immediately with your production team.

Equipment Stewardship

Cameras, lenses, mics, and switchers are high-value industry tools. Sign out all gear properly, use camera straps, and return batteries to chargers.

Cell Phone & Device Policy

Personal phones remain silenced and stored away during instruction and studio filming unless explicitly approved as a production monitor or prop.

Set Safety & Respect

Maintain quiet on active sets when red recording lights are on. Treat classmates, guests, and school property with full professionalism.

PROGRESSIVE ACCOUNTABILITY

Discipline & Behavior Consequences

Floyd County Schools Code of Conduct
Step / Offense Studio & Classroom Action Documentation & Follow-Up
STEP 01 // 1st Offense Verbal redirection and private crew conference with Mr. Williams. In-class expectation review; immediate return to task.
STEP 02 // 2nd Offense Temporary reassignment from studio equipment / production floor to written alternative assignment. Parent/Guardian contact via ParentSquare or phone call.
STEP 03 // 3rd Offense Detention / formal behavioral reflection plan and loss of independent field pass privileges. Parent conference and documented behavior log.
STEP 04 // Severe / Repeated Immediate administrative office referral (also applies immediately to intentional equipment misuse or safety violations). Handled per Floyd County Schools College & Career Academy administration.

Rubrics & Portfolios

Every major video, audio, and broadcast assignment is graded using a transparent production rubric shared prior to filming. Rubrics evaluate storytelling clarity, framing and focus, clean audio levels, pacing, and adherence to deadlines.

Students archive all approved final exports inside their personal Google Drive & Digital Portfolio so work can be reviewed during semester checks and compiled into their senior demo reel.

Communication

Open communication keeps every production running smoothly. Students check Google Classroom daily for call sheets, script deadlines, and rubric feedback.

Parents, guardians, and community partners may reach Mr. Rick Williams anytime via ParentSquare or by emailing rickwilliams@floydboe.net.
