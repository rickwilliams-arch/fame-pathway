Unsolved and Unseen

The podcast talks about different cold cases and paranormal events that still don't have answers. It goes into stories about missing people, strange deaths, creepy experiences, and things that people can't really explain. The hosts discuss the evidence, what witnesses have said, and different theories about what could have happened. Some cases seem like they could have a normal explanation, while others are just strange and leave you wondering. I found the topic interesting because it shows how many mysteries are still out there and how some questions may never get answered. It also makes you think about whether everything has an explanation or if there are some things we just don't understand.

PARANORMAL REDDIT STORIES // RATED 1–5

Fright Factor

3rd Tuesday
Paranormal & Scary Stories 3rd Tuesday of the Month

Fright Factor

Step into the unexplained with a paranormal podcast where two co-hosts explore one chilling Reddit story in every episode. From mysterious encounters and ghostly sightings to unsettling experiences that defy explanation, each story is put under the spotlight. Together, the hosts discuss the details, question the evidence, and debate whether the events could actually be real. At the end of each episode, they rate the story on a scale of one to five on how believable and terrifying it is. Tune in, judge the stories for yourself, and decide whether you're listening to someone's imagination or something truly paranormal.

Investigative & Business Deep-Dive Through May 2027

Catching Corrupt Corporations

Our podcast is called “Catching Corrupt Corporations”. Once a month, we research a different corporation and expose the truths about it. We discover the pros and cons of each corporation and explain how we feel they are both helping and hurting people. Our goal is to raise awareness of what the problems are with corporations in the world today, hopefully encouraging them to fix them. You can watch our episodes on the last Tuesday of every month up until May, 2027.

4th Thursday

Off the Records

Welcome to Off the Record, a monthly sports podcast hosted by four student-athletes covering everything from high school athletics to the college and professional ranks. Each month, we spotlight all of our school's teams across every season, recapping standout games and recognizing dedicated student-athletes. As active competitors ourselves, we bring authentic player perspectives, informed analysis, and behind-the-scenes stories to every conversation. Beyond our own campus, we also share thoughtful takes on the biggest headlines, rivalries, and matchups across college and pro sports. Tune in each month as we take the conversation Off the Record.
