The FAME Podcast Studio

01 / BROADCAST AUDIO

Rodecaster Pro II

Students engineer multi-track studio audio with broadcast dynamic microphones, real-time EQ, sound pads, and crisp vocal processing.

02 / MULTI-CAM VIDEO

Canon Mirrorless & ATEM
