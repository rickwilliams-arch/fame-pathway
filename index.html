About The FAME Pathway

"FAME empowers students to step behind the lens, take command of the control room, and bring original stories from script to screen."

Located at the Floyd County College & Career Academy (CCA), the FAME (Film, Arts, Media, and Entertainment) pathway prepares aspiring filmmakers, broadcast journalists, editors, and digital content creators for real-world media production.

Rather than learning solely from textbooks, FAME operates like an active production studio. Students collaborate on live school broadcasts, narrative short films, promotional videos, and community storytelling projects using industry-standard cameras, lighting rigs, audio gear, and non-linear editing suites.

01. Pre-Production & Writing

[SCRIPT]

Screenwriting, storyboarding, shot listing, casting, and creative pitching to turn raw ideas into actionable production plans.

02. Cinematography & Lighting

[CAMERA]

Mastering camera movement, framing, exposure, three-point studio lighting, and cinematic visual composition.

03. Broadcast & Live Media

[ON AIR]

Multi-camera studio switching, on-camera anchoring, field reporting, audio mixing, and live event production.

04. Post-Production & Sound

[EDIT]

Non-linear video editing, color grading, Foley sound design, motion graphics, and festival-ready mastering.

Meet The Teacher

Film • Arts • Media • Entertainment

Rick Williams

"Every student walks into the studio with a unique voice. My job is to give them the tools, confidence, and craft to share it with the world."

As the instructor for the FAME Pathway at the Floyd County College & Career Academy, Mr. Rick Williams mentors students through every stage of creative media production—from brainstorming initial concepts and writing scripts to directing crews on set and polishing final cuts in post-production.

With a focus on real-world collaboration, technical excellence, and creative problem-solving, Mr. Williams guides students as they build professional portfolios, produce authentic broadcasts for the school community, and discover career pathways in film, television, journalism, and digital media.
