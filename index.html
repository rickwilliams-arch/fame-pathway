About

FAME

The Floyd County College & Career Academy’s FAME (Film, Arts, Media & Entertainment) Pathway is an award-winning, project-based program founded in 2020 that immerses students in the full spectrum of film production. From scriptwriting and cinematography to lighting, sound, and editing, students gain hands-on experience creating high-quality content for film festivals, competitions, and social media platforms.

FAME emphasizes not only technical skills but also 21st-century competencies such as confidence, connection, creativity, and community. Students learn to tell stories that resonate both on screen and online, building real-world experience in digital media and audience engagement. Guided by instructor Rick Williams, who brings over 20 years of professional experience across multiple industries, the pathway prepares students to thrive in modern media careers and beyond.

01 / FILMING

Filming

Master narrative and documentary filmmaking from script to screen, combining cinema camera operation, dramatic lighting, location audio, and festival-ready editing.

02 / PODCASTING

Podcasting

Produce studio-grade audio and video podcasts by developing vocal mic technique, multi-track mixing, interviewing skills, and engaging episodic storytelling.

03 / DIGITAL

Content Creation

Craft high-impact short-form videos, vlogs, and digital media tailored for modern social platforms through fast-paced editing and authentic storytelling.

04 / IDENTITY

Branding

Build cohesive visual identities, media kits, and promotional campaigns using the FAME Formula to connect creative work with real-world audiences.

FAME Pathway Instructor

Rick Williams

Rick Williams is an award-winning film educator and media innovator based in Rome, Georgia. With over two decades of experience across various industries, he has become a trailblazer in integrating real-world media production into high school curricula. As the instructor of the Film, Arts, Media & Entertainment (FAME) Pathway at the Floyd County College & Career Academy, Rick created the FAME Formula—the signature framework he used to transform FAME into a high-powered, student-led production program blending filmmaking, podcasting, content creation, and digital branding.

Recognized for his impact in the classroom and beyond, Rick was named the 2025-2026 CCA Teacher of the Year and honored with the 2025 GaETC Rising Star Award. A sought-after presenter at major conferences—including as a 2025 GaETC Featured Speaker, 2024 ACTE Speaker, and 2024 Camp T & I Speaker—he shares the FAME Formula with educators across Georgia and the nation, demonstrating how to empower students to take creative ownership of their learning.

He has also partnered with the Georgia Film Academy to design and lead statewide teacher training programs focused on podcasting, vlogging, and integrating media production into existing curricula, fostering a new generation of media-savvy students and educators.
