SCENE 01 // THE CURRICULUM

The FAME Pathway

Hands-on, industry-aligned training in Audio-Video Technology & Film at the Floyd County College & Career Academy.

LEVEL 01 // FOUNDATIONS

Audio & Video Tech I

Master camera operation, three-point studio lighting, field audio capture, scriptwriting, storyboarding, and non-linear video editing fundamentals.

LEVEL 02 // STUDIO PRODUCTION

Audio & Video Tech II

Step into live broadcast newsroom roles, multi-cam switching, podcast engineering, motion graphics, and narrative short film production.

LEVEL 03 // EXECUTIVE CREW

Audio & Video Tech III

Lead student production teams, produce client commercials for local Floyd County sponsors, build a demo reel, and earn industry credentials.
