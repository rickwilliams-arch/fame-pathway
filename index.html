FCS Flash News

“Your schools. Your stories. Your news.”

FCS Flash News is FAME’s fast-paced weekly news show bringing students, staff, and families the latest happenings across Floyd County Schools—dropping on Instagram every Friday. From school events and student achievements to important announcements and community highlights, Flash News keeps you connected to what’s happening throughout our district. Produced by FAME students, it’s local school news with a student perspective.

The Teacher’s Lounge with Mr. Williams

“Professional development in disguise.”

Pull up a chair and join Mr. Williams in The Teacher’s Lounge, where the everyday realities of teaching meet practical advice, fresh perspectives, and a healthy dose of humor. From classroom management and motivating students before a long vacation break to surviving a surprise sunrise administrative observation, Mr. Williams tackles the situations educators know all too well. Each episode blends proven classroom strategies, relatable experiences, and a little comedy to offer ideas you can actually use. Because sometimes the best professional development doesn’t feel like professional development at all.

FAME in the Spotlight

“One Minute. One Story.”

Every educator has a story worth sharing. FAME in the Spotlight shines a light on the incredible teachers and staff who make Floyd County Schools special. In quick, one-minute features, we go beyond the classroom to highlight the personalities, passions, talents, and stories of the people who make a difference in our schools every day. It’s our way of celebrating the people behind the impact.
